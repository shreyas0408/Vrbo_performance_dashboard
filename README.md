# Vrbo Business Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing Vrbo listings, host growth,
Superhost distribution, pricing and guest ratings across cities.

## Business Questions
- How did new listings and hosts grow over time?
- Which cities hold the most listings and reviews?
- Which cities are rated best and worst, and in which categories?
- How does price differ by room type?

## Dashboard Pages
1. **Overview** – KPI cards (listings, cities, hosts, property types,
   reviews) and a yearly trend of new listings by room type.
2. **Ratings** – Superhost Pareto by city, average price by room type,
   average rating by city, and a detailed table of 5 rating categories
   (accuracy, communication, cleanliness, location, value).
   Bookmarks switch between the Overview and Detailed views.

## Key Findings
- New listings peaked in 2015, slowed in 2016-2017, and grew again
  until 2019.
- Paris, NYC and Sydney hold almost half of all listings and 48%
  of reviews.
- Mexico and Rio are the best-rated cities; Hong Kong and Istanbul
  are the lowest.
- Cleanliness and value for money are the lowest-scoring categories.

## Tools & Skills
- Power BI Desktop
- DAX measures (averages, cumulative %, Superhost counts)
- Data modelling (Listings and Reviews tables)
- Pareto analysis, bookmarks, custom theme
